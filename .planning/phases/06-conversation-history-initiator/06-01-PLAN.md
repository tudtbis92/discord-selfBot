    5. `formatForPrompt(channelId: string, maxEntries?: number): string` — Read from RAM cache (no async), take last N entries (default 20), format as `Name: content` lines joined by `\n` (per D-06).

    - D-05: Last 15-20 messages
    - D-06: Format `Name: content`
